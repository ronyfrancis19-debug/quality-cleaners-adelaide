What’s included

Handover is smoother when the property is presented well. We provide thorough end-of-lease and exit cleaning for Adelaide homes, including premium exit cleans carried out for Defence Housing Australia.

  • A thorough clean of kitchens, bathrooms, living areas, and bedrooms
  • Stovetops, ovens, and the interiors of cupboards or appliances where they are part of the booking
  • Windows, skirting, fixtures, and other detail work that is easy to miss in a regular clean
  • Floors throughout, and carpet shampooing if it is included in the booking
  • Outdoor entries and patios tidied where they form part of the property handover

Tell us the property, the timing, and any focus areas — kitchens, bathrooms, carpets, or a full exit clean — and we will take it from there.
